## Adjudication rules (applied identically to all four approaches)

1. **Generic labels earn no credit.** "vegetables", "side salad", "legumes", "toppings",
   "sushi platter" name nothing; itemized gold rows stay misses. (Named dishes with
   determinate composition DO consolidate: "Greek salad", "pho" → beef, "paella" → prawns.)
2. **Species/kind errors are misses; prep/cut/form errors are hits.** chicken↔fish,
   meat↔legume, dairy↔legume = miss. ribs↔chops, deviled↔spiced egg, croquette↔tater tot,
   whole fish↔fish cake = hit. Organ identity counts as kind: "beef stew" for liver or
   tripe = miss.
3. **A misnamed visible object is never a hallucination** — hallucination requires a food
   with no referent in the photo. Items visible only on menus/posters score per the gold
   `trap` field (47's menu-card items are hallucinations by gold definition).
4. **Sashimi for nigiri is a miss** (rice + form lost) — the exact granularity lie the
   worksheet protocol calls out.

## The four findings

1. **Local Qwen3-VL-8B Q4 ties the cloud frontier baseline: 74.5% vs 75.3% is inside one
   image of noise, and both have ZERO hallucinations across 50 plates.** The 10-image set's
   "local Q4 lost ~12 points to cloud" finding does not replicate at scale. This is the
   quality case for the hosted tier closed: self-hosting Qwen3-VL-8B gives cloud-frontier
   identification quality; the only remaining gap is latency, which is the GPU's job.
2. **The LFM ensemble thesis is dead at this model scale.** n=3 + judge yields 66% recall
   with 38 hallucinations and 18 dedup failures — only +4 recall points over the single
   1.6B model while multiplying its hallucinations ×3 and taking 4.8× the wall time. Its
   apparent 88% on the 10-image set was small-sample noise, as suspected in the 2026-08-11
   scoring. The pipeline-v3 candidate work remains useful for token discipline, but the
   product architecture should assume **single strong model**, not ensemble-of-small.
3. **The ~75% ceiling is enumeration depth, not misidentification.** The frontier cloud
   model hits the same wall: models report 3.6–4.8 items/plate against a gold mean of 4.7,
   omit condiments/components, and emit generic labels where gold itemizes. Recall gains
   from here come from prompt-side "name every distinct component" pressure (v3 territory),
   not from bigger models.
4. **Both zero-hallucination approaches are the two single strong models.** Hallucination
   correlates with model size and with the ensemble merge, not with local-vs-cloud. For a
   nutrition product, qwen's failure mode (misses, honest generic labels) is far safer than
   the ensemble's (invented foods).

## Latency reality check (CPU, full-res, v2)

qwen single 94 s median / 193 s p95 — 10–20× over the 10 s product SLO, as modeled in
PERFORMANCE.md. Nothing new: decode is memory-bandwidth-bound and this workstation has
~70 GB/s. The v3 terse format + 896 px downscale projections (≈7 s on a ~1000 GB/s GPU for
single qwen) are the numbers the Runpod 4090 test must validate.
